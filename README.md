Entry for [The Painfully Honest Out-of-Office](https://make.fun/c/u-po-pu-the-painfully-honest-out-of-office-63e7ca0b) on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.py` deterministically writes one out-of-office email auto-reply
(191 characters, one sentence) to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no
randomness, no clock — two runs produce byte-identical output.

The reply:

> I am out of the office until Monday, and honestly I am using this holiday to avoid an inbox I cannot face, but I promise your message will be the first I read with a clear head when I return.

Run it with `python3 main.py`.
